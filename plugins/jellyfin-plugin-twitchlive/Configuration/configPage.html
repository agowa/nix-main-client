One channel per line. These are Twitch logins, not display names - the part of the channel URL that comes after twitch.tv/. Changes apply as soon as they are saved; run the Refresh Guide task to move channels between the live and offline groups in the guide immediately instead of waiting for the next refresh.

Logins are matched case-insensitively. A login that does not exist stays in the offline group, and the guide says so.
